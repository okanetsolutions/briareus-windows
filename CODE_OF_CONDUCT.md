# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, is expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith and ask before judging.
- Keep feedback specific, actionable and about the work.
- Welcome newcomers and help them find their way.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, publishing others' private information and sustained disruption are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently block anyone who repeatedly breaks it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (the repository's Security tab or a direct message to a maintainer). Reports are handled confidentially.
